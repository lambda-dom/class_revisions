# A. Changes.

This component does a few tweaks to Mage HLA's. First, it deduplicates the Mage HLA tables. It tweaks the Extra Spell HLA's in the following ways: extra 6th spell now gives 1 extra spell at levels 1 to 4, extra 7th spell gives an extra spell at levels 5 to 7 and extra 7th spell gives one extra spell at levels 8 and 9. Finally, it also changes the Summon Planetar duration to 2 turns.
